How E-Report Works

New to E-Report? Here's a quick overview of how to submit, track, and resolve IDN reports in just a few simple steps.

Start a Report Back to Home
Submit a Report

Fill out the report form with a category, a short description, and an optional photo — it only takes a minute, and you can stay anonymous if you prefer.

We Review It

Our team reviews each report and routes it to the right department, usually within 1–3 business days depending on urgency.

Track the Resolution

Follow the status of your report and see it marked resolved right on the Recent Reports page.

Tips for a Great Report

A few small things that help our team resolve your report faster.

Be specific

Mention the exact location, time, and what happened so the right team can act quickly.

Attach a photo

A clear photo helps us understand the issue and confirm it's been resolved.

Use Anonymous wisely

Choose Anonymous when you need to, but sharing your name helps us follow up if more details are needed.

Check the category

Picking the right category routes your report to the correct department faster.

Ready to submit your first report?

It only takes a minute, and you can always check its status from the Recent Reports page.
