Trash Buildup in Front of the Canteen

By Dimas | 1 day ago

The trash bin located in front of the canteen has been overflowing for the past three days. Students and staff have reported a strong, unpleasant smell during lunch hours, especially when the weather is warm. The pile of waste has also started to attract flies and other pests, raising concerns about hygiene in the dining area.

"Every time I walk past the canteen, the smell is unbearable. I hope this gets picked up soon," said one student who reported the issue.

The cleaning staff has been notified and a pickup is expected within the next 24 hours. This report will be updated once the issue has been resolved.
